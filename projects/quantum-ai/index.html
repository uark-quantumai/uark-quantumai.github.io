Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping industries, while Quantum Computing promises to revolutionize the very foundations of computation. Quantum AI sits at the convergence of these two transformative fields, exploring how quantum mechanics can accelerate, enhance, and fundamentally reimagine intelligent systems. By leveraging quantum principles such as superposition, entanglement, and tunneling, Quantum AI seeks to overcome classical bottlenecks in optimization, learning, and reasoning. This emerging field envisions AI models that learn faster, search deeper, and adapt more intelligently—capabilities that are critical for solving complex challenges in science, healthcare, finance, logistics, and beyond.
